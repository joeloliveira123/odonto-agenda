# Odonto Agenda — Clinical Content Guidelines

1. **Educational Purpose Only:** Treatment guides and Odonto AI responses are strictly informational and never substitute for an in-person dental evaluation.
2. **No Guarantees or Fabricated Claims:** Never promise painlessness, exact aesthetic outcomes, or fixed recovery times; avoid invented CRO numbers, reviews, or equipment.
3. **Publication Gate:** Only `TreatmentArticle` and `FAQ` records with `publicationStatus === "published"` and dentist reviewer attribution (`reviewerStaffId` + `reviewedAt`) are rendered publicly.
4. **Emergency Escalation:** Acute pain, fever, or progressive swelling must direct patients to immediate in-person/medical care and reception contact—never automated clinical triage.
